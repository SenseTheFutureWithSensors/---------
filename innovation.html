04 — Methodology

The 4-Step Sensor
Innovation Framework.

Every breakthrough sensor invention follows a systematic four-stage engineering lifecycle.

STEP 01

Identify Physical Signal

Observe the physical world. Where is energy, pressure, temperature, movement, or chemical composition changing in ways that signal a problem or an inefficiency?

STEP 02

Select & Condition

Pick the transducer that best balances sensitivity, operating temperature, power consumption, and cost. Condition the raw signal through filtering and precision ADC quantization.

STEP 03

Apply Edge Intelligence

Run calibration algorithms, digital filters, moving averages, or TinyML models on an ESP32 or Raspberry Pi to extract meaningful insights from noisy raw sensor readings.

STEP 04

Close Loop for Impact

Don't stop at dashboards. Trigger real-world actuators: turn on pumps, sound emergency sirens, alert doctors, or divert traffic to solve the problem in real-time.
